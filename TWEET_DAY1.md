## Notes on posting

- **Best time:** Tuesday-Thursday 8-10am PT (your X audience is mostly US dev/builder)
- **Pin the tweet** to your profile until v0.1 launches
- **First reply (yourself):** explain the BrandGain context if Draft A
- **Hashtags:** none. Hashtags lower reach on X in 2026
- **Image:** screenshot of Chief landing or BUILD_JOURNAL.md Day 1 entry

---

## What to do if it gets traction (>50 likes Day 1)

- Reply with screenshots of progress every 2 days
- DM the warmest engagers — invite to alpha
- Pin a thread reply with email signup → drives waitlist

## What to do if it gets zero traction

- Don't sweat. Day 1 with no audience = expected.
- Focus on building. Tweet again Day 3 with progress shots.
- Build the audience in parallel with the product. By week 8 (Show HN), you should have 50-200 followers minimum.
